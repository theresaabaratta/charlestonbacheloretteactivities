Best Time to Visit Charleston for a Bachelorette Party

Weather, crowds, prices, and events — broken down season by season so you can pick the right weekend for your girls' trip.

The Short Answer

Late September through November, and late March through April, are the sweet spot. You get warm afternoons, cool evenings, lighter humidity, and enough daylight for a rooftop crawl without melting your makeup by 2pm.

Charleston, Season by Season

Top Pick

Fall (Sep–Nov)

Mid-70s to low-80s by day, crisp in the evenings. Crowds thin out after Labor Day, hotel rates drop, and rooftop bars are perfect at sunset. Best window for photos on the Battery without a sea of other tourists in the frame.

Top Pick

Spring (Mar–May)

Azaleas and jasmine everywhere, temps in the 70s to low 80s. Wine + Food Festival in March and Spoleto in late May add energy, but book early — this is also wedding and prom season downtown.

Peak Heat

Summer (Jun–Aug)

Highs in the upper 80s to low 90s with heavy humidity. Great if the plan revolves around the beach, a boat day, or the pool — less great for a lot of walking downtown in matching outfits.

Budget Pick

Winter (Dec–Feb)

Mild by most standards — 50s and 60s — and the cheapest hotel rates of the year. Fewer outdoor rooftop options are in full swing, but restaurants and bars are easier to book without a huge waitlist.

A Few Things to Plan Around

Hurricane Season

Runs June through November, with August and September the highest-risk months. Charleston rarely takes a direct hit, but if you're booking a fall date, keep an eye on the forecast the week before and consider travel insurance.

Big Local Events

Cooper River Bridge Run (April), Charleston Wine + Food Festival (March), and Spoleto Festival (late May–June) fill up hotels fast. Great energy if you want to be part of it, but book months ahead.

Weekday vs. Weekend

Friday–Saturday downtown gets loud and crowded regardless of season. A Thursday–Sunday trip in shoulder season gets you the best of both: fewer crowds, lower rates, still plenty of nightlife.
