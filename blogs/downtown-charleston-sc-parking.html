The Basics

Downtown is a small historic peninsula with narrow streets and limited spaces, so parking fills fast and gets expensive. There are four options: garages, street meters, metered lots and residential streets with time limits.

How Residential Parking Works

Many downtown streets are residential parking districts. Residents with a permit can park with no time limit. Everyone else follows the posted limit.

For Visitors: The 2-Hour Signs

The Rule

No permit? Park only for the posted limit (usually 2 hours; some streets 1 or 4) during the days and hours on the sign.

Enforcement

Permits are digital and plates are scanned. Don't count on a grace period.

Staying Longer

Get a one-day visitor pass from the resident you're visiting, or use a garage.

Ticket Traps

Wrong-direction parking, yellow curbs, outside painted lines, street-sweeping signs.

How to Apply

  • Online: parkcharleston-sc.com/permit (active on approval; $2.50 card fee per item)
  • Mail: Application, documents and check or money order to Revenue Collections, 180 Lockwood Blvd., Suite 234, Charleston, SC 29403
  • In person: By appointment

Need to Know

  • Unpaid tickets must be paid before a permit is issued.
  • Visitor booklets are mail or in-person only, cash or check.
  • A car that hasn't moved in 7 days can be considered abandoned, even with a permit.
  • Questions: 843-724-7375.

Monthly Parking

Downtown workers and residents without a driveway often pay monthly. The City has no monthly vacancies and no waiting list. County garages offer 24/7 monthly access:

Tips for Visitors & Off-Peninsula Locals

  • Park once. Use CARTA's free DASH shuttle to get around downtown.
  • Come after 5. $5 evening rates at Majestic, East Bay/Prioleau and 93 Queen; meters free after 6.
  • Sunday is easiest. Meters and metered lots are free.
  • Ask your hotel about overnight parking costs before booking.
  • Tall vehicle? Check the city's garage height clearances first.
  • Pay tickets on time to avoid late fees.
