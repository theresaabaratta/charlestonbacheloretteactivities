Folly Beach Bachelorette

A simple rundown of where to eat, what to do on the sand, and where the whole group can sleep — no fuss, just the good stuff.

Eats

Where to Eat

Folly keeps it casual — flip-flops-at-the-table casual — and these are the spots that feed a group without anyone needing a dinner reservation weeks out.

Breakfast

Lost Dog Cafe

The go-to morning stop before a beach day, known for big portions and a laid-back patio that fits a group.

Seafood

Chico Feo

An open-air spot with picnic tables, cheap tacos, and cold drinks — the easiest lunch for a group that doesn't want to sit still long.

Dinner

Rita's Seaside Grille

A solid sit-down dinner option right off the main strip, with a bar area that's easy to gather the whole party around.

Drinks

Snapper Jack's Bar & Grill

Rooftop-adjacent, always lively, and a reliable place to post up for happy hour before the night gets going.

Late Night

Jack of Cups Saloon

Folly's dive-bar staple — live music some nights, no pretense, and open late for whoever's still standing.

Sweet Stop

Cool Runnings Ice Cream

A short walk from the pier and the easiest way to end a beach afternoon on a sweet note.

Beach & Activities

What to Do

The beach itself does most of the work, but here's what fills the rest of the day.

Beach Day

Folly Beach County Park

Quieter than the main strip, with wide sand and easy parking — a good home base for setting up chairs for the day.

Landmark

Folly Beach Pier

Walkable, photogenic, and worth a sunset stroll no matter how the rest of the itinerary shakes out.

On the Water

Paddleboard or Kayak Rental

Several rental spots near the Folly River side make it easy to get the group out on the water for an hour or two.

Learn Something

Surf Lesson

Folly's known as the Edge of America for surfers — a beginner group lesson is an easy, low-stakes group activity.

Shopping

Center Street Shops

A few blocks of boutiques and surf shops for casual browsing between the beach and dinner.

Golf Cart

Golf Cart Rental

A popular way to get the group around the island without needing a car for every outing.

Places to Stay

Where to Sleep

Most groups do better in a house than a hotel block — more room to actually hang out together.

Group House

Vacation Rental on Folly

Beach houses near the county park or the pier tend to book fastest — look for one with a porch big enough for the whole crew.

Hotel

Tides Folly Beach

The island's main hotel option, oceanfront with a rooftop bar — a good pick if the group prefers separate rooms over one big house.

Nearby Option

Downtown Charleston Stay

Some groups split the trip — a night or two downtown, a night or two on Folly — and stay somewhere central for the drive over.
