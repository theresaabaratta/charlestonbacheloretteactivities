Who to party with and where to go

Activities For Your Charleston Bachelorette Party.

When you think “bachelorette party”, there is a 100% chance that Charleston SC will come to mind. With its endless pool parties at Palma, world-renowned resturants, and King Street nightlife, it’s the absolute perfect location for a girls weekend away.

Cobblestone streets, low-country cocktails, and a harbor made for a golden-hour toast — The Holy City is one of the best bachelorette destinations in the South. Here's the local, unsponsored rundown of where to eat, what to do, where to crash, and who to call when you'd rather not lift a finger.

I've explored Charleston more times than I can count and helped plan two bachelorette weekends here. So if you're planning your bestie's bachelorette party in Charleston, here's your complete itinerary — from how to kick things off right, to the farewell brunch guaranteed to cure even the gnarliest hangover.

Sample weekend

A 4-Day Charleston Bach Itinerary

A fuller weekend than a quick in-and-out — arrive Thursday, ease in, and give the group a real mix of water, food, and downtime before everyone flies home Sunday.

Day One

Thursday

  • Afternoon Check into your East Bay rowhouse rental — decor already set up by Southern Soirée
  • Evening Low-key dinner at the house while stragglers land
  • Night Porch drinks and a group toast to kick off the weekend

Day Two

Friday

  • Morning Brunch at Basic Kitchen
  • Afternoon High Wire Distilling Tour, then a Paint & Sip class
  • Evening Dinner at The Ordinary
  • Night Drinks at The Watch Rooftop

Day Three

Saturday

  • Morning Sunrise paddle at Shem Creek
  • Afternoon Charleston Culinary Walking Tour
  • Evening Shem Creek walk and eat at Reds
  • Night Charleston Escape Room Challenge, then out on the town

Day Four

Sunday

  • Morning Slow start with spa treatments at Woodhouse
  • Midday Farewell brunch and last photos on the porch
  • Afternoon Pack up, check out, and head to the airport

Build in at least one unscheduled block per day — the best bach weekends leave room to wander.

Remember this

Charleston is best for brides who...

🌊 Love a beach day

Base yourselves downtown and take a 25-minute ride out to Sullivan's Island or Isle of Palms for a laid-back beach afternoon between parties.

🍸 Live for a good cocktail

Charleston's bar scene runs deep — think barrel-aged old fashioneds and rooftop lounges with harbor views.

🦪 Appreciate real seafood

Fresh-shucked oysters, she-crab soup, and dockside dining are non-negotiable on a Charleston bach weekend.

🏛️ Geek out on history

Cobblestone streets, Rainbow Row, and centuries-old churches make the daytime hours just as memorable as the nights.

House One

Party Planners & Decor

For the maid of honor who'd rather not build a balloon arch at midnight.

Decor & Setup

Last Fling Charleston

Luxury Airbnb decor packages, custom mimosa bars, and VIP planning add-ons for a rental that photographs as good as it feels.

Estimated cost: $865–$1,465 per party of 6 (package-based)

Full-Service

Southern Soirée

A Charleston-born party planner and decorator known for custom sugar cookies and thoughtful, personal touches on every setup.

Estimated cost: Packages start around $350, custom-quoted by group size and theme

Concierge

Bienvenue Charleston

Balloon garlands, Instagram-ready backdrops, and fridge-stocking service so your group can walk into a fully styled house.

Estimated cost: Roughly $400–$1,000+ depending on package (comparable to similar local decor services — confirm with a quote)

We are not affiliated with, and receive no compensation from, any planner listed here.

House Two

Things To Do

From harbor cruises to candle-making, here's how to fill the daylight hours.

On The Water

Shem Creek Sunset Kayak Tour

Mount Pleasant

Paddle the saltmarsh at golden hour as the shrimp boats come in — a quieter, more scenic alternative to a booze cruise.

Estimated cost: $45–$65 per person

Tasting

High Wire Distilling Tour

Old Naval Base / NoMo

A guided tasting through Charleston's own small-batch distillery, featuring spirits made from heirloom Southern grains.

Estimated cost: $10 per person for the tour; tastings and cocktails extra

Creative

Michael At Your Place

Charleston Bachelorette Live Show

A interactive or not so much per your preference show. Get a fun show from a talented dancer.

Estimated cost: $200 flat rate for a 90-minute show

Food Tour

Charleston Culinary Walking Tour

French Quarter

A guided tasting crawl through historic Charleston eateries, with the stories behind the dishes served up alongside them.

Estimated cost: $85–$95 per person

Wellness

Charleston Harbor Resort

Mount Pleasant

Book the group in for facials and massages the morning after a big night out — the bride will thank you.

Estimated cost: $130–$180 per person for a 50–80 minute treatment

Puzzle Room

Charleston Escape Room Challenge

Upper King

A themed hour of puzzle-solving with the crew before the night out — a fun, low-key way to kick off a Friday.

Estimated cost: $30–$40 per person

Shopping

King Street Shopping

King Street

Stroll down downtown's historic strip for chic local boutiques and retail therapy between the bigger group activities.

Estimated cost: Free to browse — plan $50–$200+ per person if you're buying

Workshop

Candlefish Candle-Making Class

King Street

Create custom, take-home fragrances at Candlefish — a relaxed, hands-on hour that makes a great favor for the whole group.

Estimated cost: $60 per person

History

Historic & Ghost Tours

Historic District

Take a private horse-drawn carriage ride by day or an evening ghost tour through the historic district for something a little spookier.

Estimated cost: $35–$50 per person (public tour); $250–$325 per group for a private carriage

Book tours and classes 2–3 months ahead for peak spring and fall weekends.

House Three

Where To Eat & Drink

Low-country classics, rooftop pours, and one seriously good bowl of grits.

Brunch

Basic Kitchen

King Street

Bright, plant-forward brunch spot with a killer avocado toast and a rotating list of fresh-pressed cocktails — perfect for easing the crew into day two.

Estimated cost: $15–$30 per person

Dancing

Vern's

Meeting Street

Craft cocktails upstairs, a moody speakeasy-style bar below — the kind of spot that quietly turns into the best part of the night.

Estimated cost: $80–$120 per person with drinks

Fine Dining

The Ordinary

Upper King

An old bank vault turned oyster hall. Order the raw bar tower and split a bottle of something bubbly for the bride.

Estimated cost: $60–$100+ per person, raw bar towers run extra

Treats

Sweet Palm Coffee

King Street

House-made coffees, a breakfast to lunch menu, and plenty of sweets. Chicken Ceasar wrap is the best.

Estimated cost: $10–$20 per person

Rooftop

Rooftop Crawl

Downtown

Hop between trendy rooftop spots like Stars Rooftop & Grill Room, The Cocktail Club, and The Watch Rooftop Kitchen for a moving happy hour with harbor views.

Estimated cost: $40–$70 per person for drinks and shared bites across all three stops

Brunch

Boozy Brunches

Downtown

Grab iconic Southern comfort breakfast items at Callie's Hot Little Biscuit or Welton's Famous Bakery before the day's activities kick off.

Estimated cost: $10–$20 per person

Fine Dining

Celebration Dinners: Oak Steakhouse

Broad Street

A classic steakhouse in a historic building — the pick for a proper celebration dinner with the whole crew dressed up.

Estimated cost: $80–$150+ per person with drinks

All picks are our own — no restaurant paid to be featured on this list.

House Four

Where To Stay

Group-friendly stays that keep everyone under one roof.

Boutique Hotel

The Vendue

French Quarter

An art-filled boutique hotel two blocks from the water, with a rooftop bar that makes a great home-base meeting point.

Estimated cost: $250–$450+ per night, per room

Historic Inn

HarbourView Inn

East Bay Street

Wake up to a harbor view and walk everywhere from here — the Market, Rainbow Row, and King Street are all under ten minutes on foot.

Estimated cost: $250–$500+ per night, per room

Group Rental

East Bay Rowhouse Rentals

East Bay Street

Multi-bedroom historic rowhomes that sleep 8–12, so nobody has to split off into separate hotel rooms.

Estimated cost: $700–$1,200+ per night for the whole house (split across the group)

Book your group stay 3+ months out — downtown weekends fill up fast, especially in fall wedding season.
