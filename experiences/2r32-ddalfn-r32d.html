The Tiny Tassel Private Shopping Party

An after-hours shopping party at a handmade jewelry and clothing boutique, one block off King Street.

WhenMon–Sat, 5–6:30 pm
Deposit$50, back as a gift card
Where46 Spring St, Unit B

About this experience

The Tiny Tassel closes its Charleston flagship to the public and opens it to your group. Their team styles the crew one-on-one while you browse handmade tassel earrings, jewelry, dresses and accessories, with complimentary zero-proof drinks.

It's a good pick for the bride who'd rather leave with matching earrings than a hangover. Everything is made in-house by a women-led team, and the shop is easy to fold into a day of brunch and boutique hopping. Planning the rest of the weekend?
