Cocktail Class · At Your Rental

Nightcap Social Co. Cocktail Masterclass

A bartender comes to your Airbnb and teaches the group three craft cocktails from scratch.

PriceFrom $50/person
Group6-person minimum
LengthAbout 2.5 hours

Why it works

It's a pregame and an activity in one, and nobody has to leave the house. The crew splits into stations of 2 to 4 while the bartender walks you through each drink, shaker to garnish, plus the story behind it. Menus can be themed to the bride, and mocktails are available for anyone not drinking. For more to book around it, see our full list of Charleston bachelorette party activities.

What's included

  • A Nightcap bartender to guide the class
  • All bar equipment
  • 3 cocktails, customizable to your theme
