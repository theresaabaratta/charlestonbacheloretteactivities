Silver BRIDE & Ring Balloon Photo Backdrop

Silver BRIDE letters plus two engagement ring balloons, for a backdrop that photographs well day or night.

Why it works

Silver reads clean in flash photos, so it's a good match for a black-and-white or disco-themed weekend. The two ring balloons give you an extra prop for the bride's solo shots. Set it up behind the welcome dinner table or in the rental's living room for arrival photos. For the rest of the weekend, see our Charleston bachelorette ideas.

What's included

  • Silver foil BRIDE letter balloons
  • 2 silver ring foil balloons

Setup tips

  • Hang the letters on a wall and float the rings on either side
  • Use removable hooks so you don't lose your rental deposit
