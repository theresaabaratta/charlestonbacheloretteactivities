Craft Workshop · King Street

Craft-DIY Crafting & Mimosas Workshop

Pick a craft, grab a drink and make something with your crew above King Street.

PriceFrom $46/person
DrinksBottomless options
GroupsPackages for 8+

Why it works

Everyone picks their own project from 20+ crafts, like bracelets, hats, textured wall art and macrame, so nobody is stuck painting the same canvas. An "Artender" walks you through each step while you drink. Book the Making & Mimosas session for a brunch-style morning, or Sangria Session before dinner.
