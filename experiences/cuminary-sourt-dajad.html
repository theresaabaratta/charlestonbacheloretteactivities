Food Tour · Downtown

Charleston Culinary Tours Bachelorette Food Tour

A guided walking tour that eats its way through downtown, with a meal's worth of tastings along the way.

Public Tour$85/person
LengthAbout 2.5 hours
Stops3–4 restaurants

Why it works

It covers lunch and sightseeing in one go, so it's a good first-day plan before everyone splits off. A certified local guide walks the group through the French Quarter and the streets around the City Market, with tastings at 3 to 4 restaurants and the stories behind them. Book a private tour and they'll build the stops around your crew. For more ways to fill the weekend, see our full list of Charleston bachelorette party activities.
