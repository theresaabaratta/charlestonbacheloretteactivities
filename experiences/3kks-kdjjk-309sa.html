About this experience

Charleston Party Cat cruises past the Ravenel Bridge, Fort Sumter and the downtown skyline. Bring your drinks; they supply the captain, music, coolers, ice, water and beer pong. Book a whole boat for your group, or join a shared cruise. Planning the rest of the weekend? See our full list of Charleston bachelorette party activities.
