Distillery Tour · Upper Peninsula

High Wire Distilling Tour & Whiskey Flight 21+

A $10 behind-the-scenes tour of a Charleston craft distillery, then cocktails by the stills.

Tour$10/person
TimesThu–Sat, 12, 2, 4 & 6 pm
GroupsPrivate tours by appointment

Why it works

It's a cheap, easy afternoon stop that feels a little more grown-up than a bar crawl. The tour covers High Wire's story, the local grains and botanicals they use, and how a spirit goes from seed to bottle. Afterward, stay at the bar for cocktails or a whiskey flight with a view of the production floor. It's a short ride from Upper King, so it pairs well with dinner downtown.
