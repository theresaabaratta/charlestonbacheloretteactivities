About this experience

Holy City Picnic builds the whole scene: low tables, rugs, cushions, candles and florals styled to your colors, plus food like charcuterie boards. You show up, take the photos and eat. It's an easy daytime plan between brunch and a night out. For more ways to fill the weekend, see our Charleston bachelorette party ideas.
