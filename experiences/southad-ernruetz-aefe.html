Hat Bar · French Quarter

Southern Ruetz Custom Hat Bar

Design your own felt hat with one of their artists, drink in hand.

Group SizeSolo to big groups
DrinksOne free with each hat
Where128 Meeting St

Why it works

Everyone leaves wearing a one-of-a-kind hat, so the rest of the weekend's photos match without anyone buying a uniform. Pick a hat, then style it with wraps, feathers, pins and vintage fabric, and have it branded or burned. Matching cowgirl hats for a coastal cowgirl theme are an easy win. For more ways to fill the weekend, see our Charleston bachelorette party ideas.

What's included

  • A custom hat from one of four tiers
  • Wraps, feathers, vintage fabrics and pins
  • Branding and burning
  • A complimentary drink with each hat
  • Access to the Makers Mall, with 40+ women-owned small businesses
