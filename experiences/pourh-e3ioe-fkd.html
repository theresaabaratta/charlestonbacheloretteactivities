Why it works

It's a laid-back night out that doesn't cost the group much. Start on the outdoor deck, where shows are free and usually kick off in the early evening, then grab tickets for the Main Stage act later on. It's a local favorite on James Island, a short ride from downtown and on the way to Folly Beach. For more to plan around it, see our full list of Charleston bachelorette party activities.
