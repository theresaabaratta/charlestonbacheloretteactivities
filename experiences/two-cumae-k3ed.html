Two Cumberland Bride Outfit Shopping

A family-run Charleston boutique for the bride's white looks and the crew's going-out outfits.

PricesAffordable, under-$100 picks
For the BrideBridal outfit collection
Where455 King Street

Why it works

Forgot an outfit, or want the bride in white all weekend? Two Cumberland has a bridal outfit collection plus dresses, sets, rompers, shoes and jewelry at prices the whole group can shop. Some pieces are named after Charleston landmarks, which makes for an easy souvenir. It's on Upper King, so it slots right into a shopping afternoon before dinner. For more to fill the weekend, see our Charleston bachelorette ideas.

What to shop

  • Bridal outfits for welcome night, brunch and the night out
  • Dresses, sets and rompers for the crew
  • Shoes, jewelry, bags and hats
  • Local gifts, like candles and body care
