Airbnb Game Night · Pregame

Midnight Taboo Bachelorette Party Game 18+

The adults-only version of Taboo. Same rules, much racier words.

Players4+, in teams
Ages18+
Best ForPregame at the rental

Why it works

Split the crew into two teams, grab drinks and play before heading out for the night. Describe the word on your card without saying any of the banned words underneath, while the other team waits with the buzzer. It packs small, needs no setup, and gets everyone talking fast, even friends who just met. For more to plan around game night, see our full list of Charleston bachelorette activities.

What's in the box

  • Hundreds of double-sided adult word cards
  • Buzzer (2 AA batteries, not included)
  • Sand timer and score pad

Tip

Pack the AA batteries. Without them the buzzer is silent and the other team has to yell instead.
