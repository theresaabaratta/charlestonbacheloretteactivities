The Commodore Live Funk & Dance Night 23+

A vintage club where live funk and jazz bands keep the checkered dance floor packed.

MusicLive bands up to 5 nights
OpenNightly from 7:30 pm
Ages23 and up

Why it works

If your crew wants to actually dance instead of shouting over a DJ, this is the spot. The room still has its original jazz-era murals and leather booths from its days as A Touch of Class, the club that once hosted James Brown and Marvin Gaye. House bands and local acts play funk, soul and jazz, and the floor fills up fast on weekends. It's a short walk from Upper King, so it works as the last stop of the night.
