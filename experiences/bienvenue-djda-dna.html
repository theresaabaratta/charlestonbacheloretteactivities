Why it works

The maid of honor gets to enjoy the trip too. Tell Bienvenue where you're staying and when, and they send a custom proposal: groceries and local snacks in the fridge, balloons and banners up, and a mimosa, spritz or margarita bar set up and ready for your bottles. They also plan the rest, from dinner reservations and transportation to walking tours, and will even hold a spot in line at Chubby Fish. For more ideas to hand off to them, see our full list of Charleston bachelorette activities.

What they can handle

  • Food and drink stocking, including charcuterie and local snacks
  • Balloons, arches, banners, florals and photo displays
  • Mimosa, spritz or margarita bar setup (you supply the alcohol)
  • Dinner reservations, bottle service and custom itineraries
  • Ground transportation and walking tours
  • Beach chairs, umbrellas, towels and coolers
