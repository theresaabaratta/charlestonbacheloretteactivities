Break Out Charleston Escape Room

PriceAbout $35/person

Why it works

It's a sober, rain-proof hour that gets the group talking, especially friends from different parts of the bride's life who just met. Each game runs through multiple rooms, and your booking is just your group. Pick Wild West Heist if you're doing a cowgirl theme: rob a fortified bank and grab the golden nugget before the gunslinger gets back. There's free parking right in the plaza.
